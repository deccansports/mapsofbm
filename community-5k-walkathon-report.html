Event Overview

The Community 5K Walkathon is positioned as a high-energy neighborhood sports event with an inclusive, non-competitive walking format. The goal is to drive participation across schools, resident groups, sponsors, and wellness communities while maintaining professional event controls, visible branding, and a clear operating budget.

  • Audience: families, schools, resident groups, fitness beginners, and local businesses.
  • Course intent: safe, signed, and marshal-supported 5K route with hydration and medical coverage.
  • Activation zones: registration desk, warm-up stage, sponsor area, hydration points, and finish arch.
  • Outcome target: healthy turnout, sponsor visibility, and repeatable community event model.

Registration Details

Volunteer Planning

  • 50 volunteers divided into route marshals, hydration crew, registration, finish chute, and cleanup.
  • Minimum 1 supervisor per 10 volunteers with radio contact and escalation responsibility.
  • Mandatory briefing 60 minutes before public entry, covering route flow, child-safety awareness, and emergency roles.
  • Volunteer meal, hydration, visibility vest, and zone map issued at sign-in.
  • Reserve pool of 5 floating volunteers to cover absenteeism or emergency redeployment.

Emergency Medical / 911 Response Plan

  • One clearly marked medical tent at start/finish staffed by certified first-aid personnel.
  • One ambulance standby point with unobstructed ingress and egress for emergency vehicles.
  • Every zone lead carries a radio and printed escalation card with the exact venue address and nearest access gate.
  • If a participant collapses or shows chest pain, breathing difficulty, heat injury, or altered consciousness, the nearest marshal calls the medical lead immediately and dials 911.
  • Caller script: identify the event, exact location marker, number of patients, current condition, and best vehicle access route.
  • Route marshals create a clear access corridor and hold crowd movement until EMS assumes scene control.
  • Registration records must include emergency contacts and any disclosed medical notes for rapid reference.
