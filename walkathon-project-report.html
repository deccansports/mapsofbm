Medical Response Plan

1. Immediate Alert Any marshal, volunteer, or lead spotting a medical emergency informs the command desk by phone or runner and calls 911 immediately if the participant is unconscious, has chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding, heat distress, or a fall with possible head/neck injury.
2. Caller Script State: “This is the Community 5K Walkathon at Lake Cunningham Marina Picnic Area. We have a medical emergency. Access point is the main event check-in area near the picnic/canopy setup. A volunteer in a bright event vest will meet responders at the nearest vehicle entry.”
3. Scene Control The closest volunteers create space, pause foot traffic, keep the participant calm, and do not move the person unless there is immediate danger. A first-aid kit is brought to the scene while waiting for EMS.
4. Responder Escort One pre-assigned lead waits at the venue entrance, flags down EMS, and escorts them directly to the participant using the fastest route. Another lead clears a path at the scene.
5. Documentation & Follow-up Record the incident time, location, witness names, 911 call time, and action taken. Notify event leadership and the participant's listed emergency contact as soon as practical.

Recommended: brief all team leads on the exact address, access point, and who owns the phone list before the event starts.
